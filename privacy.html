Privacy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Series Tracker is a personal app for keeping track of the series you watch. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app stores

Your list of shows, including names, languages, seasons, episodes, your watching status, notes, dropped-episode numbers and poster links. This is stored in your own browser (local storage) on the device you use.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", the app receives your name, email address and profile picture so it can show who is signed in. It also asks for permission to use the hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive, where it keeps one backup file (series-tracker-backup.json). The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Your list is sent only to your own Google Drive. It is not sent to any server run by the developer, and the developer cannot read it.

Looking up show details

When you add a show, its name is sent to TVmaze (and to TMDB, only if you add your own TMDB key) to find the language, seasons, episodes and poster. No account information or personal details are sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or rent your data.
  • We do not run ads or analytics trackers.
  • We do not keep your data on our own servers.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in, and your list stays only in your browser.
  • Sign out from Settings at any time. This also revokes the app's access token.
  • Remove the app's access completely at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the backup by removing the app's data there.
  • Clear your browser's site data to delete the list stored on a device.

Data sources and credits

Show details and posters come from TVmaze (licensed CC BY-SA 4.0). If you add your own TMDB key, some details also come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Posters and show names belong to their respective owners. Series Tracker is not affiliated with any streaming service, studio or network.

  • Show information comes from third parties and may be incomplete or wrong.
  • Your list is stored on your device and in your own Google Drive. Keep your own export if it matters to you, because we cannot recover lost data.
